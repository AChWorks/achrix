# Contribution Agreement Policy and Draft

Policy accepted on 2026-10-01.
Draft version: draft-0.1.
Status: NON-OPERATIVE DRAFT. This document is not a signed agreement and is not ready for contributor acceptance.

## Scope and activation

Require an appropriate CLA before merging external copyrightable code into AChrix Core or other AChWorks-owned official artifacts covered by this policy. Independently maintained third-party Modules do not require our CLA merely to interoperate.

A GitHub organization/project label is not sufficient identification of the legal agreement recipient. [Issue #15](https://github.com/AChWorks/achrix/issues/15) owns selection of the actual person/entity, authority, applicable enforceable terms and consent-record procedure. Do not substitute an invented legal entity or treat a PR checkbox, this draft, or an ordinary inbound MPL grant as signed consent to extra rights.

Until activation, defer new external first-party code merges that need this agreement. Owner-authorized work may continue when the owner has sufficient rights; any existing contribution rights must be checked rather than assumed.

## Proposed terms for finalization

The final version should state these terms explicitly, with the identified legal recipient:

1. **Parties and contribution scope.** Identify the contributor and authorized recipient, the project, and intentionally submitted original contributions. Exclude material explicitly marked as third-party or not a contribution.
2. **Ownership.** The contributor retains copyright; no copyright assignment is required by default.
3. **Copyright grant.** Grant the recipient a perpetual, worldwide, non-exclusive, royalty-free, irrevocable license, subject to applicable enforceable terms, to use, reproduce, modify, prepare derivative works, display, perform, distribute and sublicense the contributions. Explicitly permit sublicensing under open-source or commercial terms, including future relicensing/dual licensing.
4. **Patent grant.** Grant rights to patent claims the contributor can license that are necessarily infringed by the contribution, alone or with the work to which it was submitted; define a narrowly scoped patent-litigation termination rule. Do not demand unrelated patent rights.
5. **Authority and provenance.** Confirm original creation or sufficient rights, including employer/corporate authorization when required. Disclose included third-party material and its license; do not promise rights the signer lacks.
6. **Voluntary submission and warranty.** Contributions are voluntary and provided without a promise of support; resolve suitable representations, disclaimers and any limits in the final reviewed agreement.
7. **No trademark or merge entitlement.** The grant transfers no project trademark rights, maintainer role, obligation to merge, or entitlement to an official release.
8. **Acceptance evidence.** Record an explicit acceptance of the exact effective agreement version and authorized identity. A later agreement update does not silently expand a previous grant.

This is a project-specific rights brief/draft, not a copy of the Apache Software Foundation ICLA and not a claim of legal enforceability. Finalization must resolve the party details and terms before activation.

## Recordkeeping and merge check

Use the simplest verified procedure that fits the first real external contribution; a hosted CLA bot is optional tooling, never a Core runtime requirement.

Keep signed agreements and unnecessary personal/employer details out of public Git. Store minimum durable consent evidence in an approved private record with access/retention rules. Link verified agreement version and contributor identity to the PR/covered contribution so maintainers can check authority before merge without exposing private records.

A DCO/Developer Certificate of Origin alone is not the additional relicensing/sublicensing grant requested here. A CLA grants neither automatic security approval nor license compatibility for third-party dependencies.
