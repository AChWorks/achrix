Agreement version: 1.0.
Policy adoption date: 2026-10-01.
Status: adopted agreement template and manual intake policy. A contribution is covered only by verified signed acceptance; this publication signs nothing on anyone's behalf.

## Agreement terms

By signing the exact version identified in your acceptance record, you agree to these terms:

1. **You and your Contribution.** You are the identified rights holder, or an identified entity acting through its authorized signatory. A Contribution is original material you deliberately submit for inclusion in the covered project, such as code, modifications or documentation. Material clearly identified as third-party material or as not offered for inclusion is excluded from this grant. The signed record identifies whether it covers named existing submissions, future covered submissions, or both.
2. **Retained ownership and copyright permission.** You keep ownership of your Contribution. You give the recipient a permanent, worldwide, non-exclusive copyright license without royalty payments, irrevocable to the extent permitted by applicable law, to use, copy, change, create derived works, display, perform and distribute it, and to sublicense those rights. This explicitly permits open-source or commercial/closed distributions, future relicensing and dual licensing of your Contribution. It does not grant rights in unrelated code or withdraw licenses already granted to existing recipients.
3. **Necessary patent permission.** You license the recipient and recipients of authorized distributions, without royalties and worldwide, to make, use, sell, offer for sale, import and otherwise exercise your Contribution, only under patent claims you may license that are necessarily practiced by the Contribution alone or combined with the project work to which you submitted it. The recipient may sublicense this limited permission with authorized distributions. This permission is intended to be permanent and irrevocable subject to the following exception: if you bring patent litigation alleging that the Contribution or that combination infringes a patent, your patent permission for the work targeted by that litigation ends when you file it. Unrelated patent claims are not included.
4. **Authority and provenance.** You state that you have sufficient rights to make these grants. If an employer or another person controls relevant rights, obtain its permission or an authorized entity signature first. Clearly identify included third-party material, its origin and license; it remains governed by its own terms. Do not claim ownership or grant permission you do not hold.
5. **Voluntary submission.** You contribute voluntarily and promise no maintenance or support. Apart from the authority/provenance statement above, your Contribution is provided as is without a promise of fitness, merchantability, title or non-infringement, to the extent permitted by applicable law. This agreement imposes no indemnity obligation.
6. **No additional project authority.** This agreement grants no project trademark permission, maintainer role, right to a merge or official-release designation. The recipient has no obligation to accept or distribute a submission. The project's separately published governance, trademark and review policies still apply.
7. **Versioned consent.** Your signature accepts the agreement text identified in the record. A later version does not expand this grant without your separate acceptance. Changes to agreement terms require a new version. Preserve a copy of the signed text for your own records.

## Signing and private records

Use a manual process until real contribution volume justifies automation:

1. Ask a maintainer in the PR for a private submission channel. Do not post signatures, private contact details or employer records publicly. A maintainer must provide an appropriate private channel and durable record location before requesting acceptance.
2. Submit a signed copy; electronic signing is allowed. Include your legal name, GitHub identity, whether you act individually or for an identified entity, authorized signatory where applicable, date, agreement version, the exact text's commit/digest, and the contribution scope. A PR checkbox or unverified account assertion is insufficient.
3. Ali Chopani owns the private consent records and may designate the maintainer performing verification. Keep the signed text, authority evidence where needed, identity verification and covered contribution links in access-controlled durable storage for as long as the project relies on those rights, subject to applicable privacy requirements. No private archive or signing service is created by publishing this policy.
4. Before merging covered external copyrightable material, the maintainer verifies the signature/identity connection, agreement version/scope, employer authority where relevant and third-party terms. If a private channel, record or sufficient authority is missing, defer that contribution's merge.
5. Record only the minimum public PR result, for example: agreement version 1.0 verified by the named maintainer for the covered GitHub identity and this PR. Retain private evidence without linking publicly accessible personal records.

Owner-authorized work may proceed when the owner has sufficient rights. An agent may check evidence but must not invent or sign a contributor's consent.

## Limits and canonical owners

A DCO alone does not supply the additional sublicensing/relicensing grant above. A signed CLA does not establish dependency compatibility, security approval or rights in another party's code. [Licensing](licensing.md) owns artifact terms; [Governance](../../GOVERNANCE.md) and [Contributing](../../CONTRIBUTING.md) own integration authority and review.

These are project-specific terms derived from the accepted project rights policy, not an ASF agreement or a representation that enforceability has been determined for every jurisdiction.
