Policy accepted by the owner on 2026-10-01.

## Scope

AChrix, AChWorks, their official names, logos and official project identity are separate from the source-code license. This policy reserves project trademark permissions to the extent of the actual rights holder's rights and applicable law. It does not claim a registered trademark, a specific legal entity, or universal enforceability.

MPL-2.0 does not grant contributor trademark rights except as necessary for license-notice obligations. [Licensing policy](docs/legal/licensing.md) owns code-license scope; this policy does not add restrictions to MPL's copying, modification or distribution permissions.

## Accurate references

Truthful descriptive references such as “compatible with AChrix” or “based on AChrix” may identify compatibility or origin without implying endorsement. Preserve required copyright/license notices, technical import paths, provenance and attribution.

Do not claim AChWorks approval, certification or official origin without authorization. A compatibility claim must have appropriate evidence; it is not an official certification.

## Forks and modified distributions

Forking remains permitted under the code license. An independently maintained distribution should clearly identify its own distributor/name and disclose that it is unofficial or modified wherever omission would imply official origin.

Do not use project names, logos, release labels, domains, badges or copied presentation to represent a fork/third-party module as the official AChrix/AChWorks release or an endorsed product. Do not fabricate official signatures or certification records.

This is not a requirement to erase historical attribution, rename every code identifier, remove legitimate dependency references, or destroy interoperability. Necessary descriptive uses and rights available under applicable law remain unaffected.

## Permission and official identity

Use of an official logo/badge or wording implying official sponsorship/endorsement requires permission from the authorized rights holder. Project governance determines who may publish the canonical release and operate official signing/update identities; possession of source code grants no such authority.

For a permission request, start with a repository Issue containing only public-safe details, or use a designated official contact when one is published. Do not post private legal records, credentials or signing material publicly.

Trademark registration, legal ownership and enforcement procedures require separate rights-holder/legal work where needed. This repository policy is not evidence that registration has happened.
