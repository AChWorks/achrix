# AChrix Governance

## Canonical authority

AChWorks retains stewardship of canonical AChrix: project direction, maintainer delegation, merge authority, official release designation and changes to official trust/update identities.

The current owner-designated legal contribution recipient and private record owner is identified in the canonical [Contribution Agreement](docs/legal/cla.md); a project/organization label alone does not establish a registered company.

Repository write access or a submitted PR does not independently authorize policy decisions or establish a person as a legal rights holder. The current explicit assignment still determines an agent's mutation scope.

Koinon owns generic cross-project governance/contracts. This file specializes project stewardship; it does not replace Koinon or make it a runtime dependency.

## Contributions and integration

Canonical tracked changes use the existing [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md) workflow and [AGENTS.md](AGENTS.md) integration/validation rules. Maintainers evaluate scope, compatibility, security, dependency licensing and the required contribution rights before integration.

External code contributions follow the [Contribution Agreement](docs/legal/cla.md); a published template without verified signed acceptance cannot satisfy the rights check. Acceptance of contributions is discretionary and never grants merge or official-release authority.

Keep implementation review and independent review distinct. Require separation where actual risk or repository policy warrants it; do not describe self-review as independent.

## Decisions and published rights

Keep project intent in `docs/MASTER-SPEC.md`, detailed rules in their canonical docs, accepted rationale in ADRs, and work/evidence in GitHub/Git/CI. Material licensing, public-contract, security or commercial commitments require the authorized owner's decision.

AChWorks may maintain commercial Modules/services under compatible terms. Future relicensing or dual licensing requires an actual rights/dependency audit and an explicit decision; existing recipients retain rights already granted by their licenses.

## Official releases and ecosystem

Only designated maintainers/release authority may label an artifact or module official. [Lifecycle and compatibility](docs/lifecycle/lifecycle-and-compatibility.md) owns release identity/evidence and future verification boundaries; [TRADEMARKS.md](TRADEMARKS.md) owns branding.

Public source, documentation and permitted forks remain available under their licenses. Independent forks own their own maintenance/representation and may reuse licensed tooling/documents; they cannot claim our release authority or control official signing keys.

Third-party modules and independently operated consumers remain legitimate ecosystem participants. An official registry, certification or company account is not a prerequisite for using Core or an authorized extension.
